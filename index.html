Electronic Change Review Board

Welcome to the Plasma OpU eCRB

A guide to identifying the right review board, submitting a Level 2 change draft, tracking endorsement, and monitoring CRB metrics.

Standalone demonstration: submissions and tracker updates are saved only in this browser. Power Automate, SharePoint, Microsoft Teams Approvals, email notifications, and Power BI are not connected in this app.

How to use the six modules

  1. Decision Matrix: Identify launch-related changes for the Global Product Launch CRB and product affiliation. Share information about Biologics or combined Biologics/Plasma products with the Bio OpU PQLs, then continue non-launch changes to the five Step 2 criteria.
  2. Submission Form: For a change routed to Plasma OpU CRB, provide the draft details and submit it to this local demo tracker.
  3. Tracker: View submitted drafts and record the CRB decision, actual CRB date, and actions.
  4. Approvals: This module explains where live approval requests would appear; actual Teams and Power Automate approval links require an integration.
  5. Metrics: Review counts and decision outcomes for drafts stored in this browser.

Endorsement guidance

Endorsers should review the change-control presentation and record one of these outcomes: Endorsed, Endorsed with actions, or Rejected. Use comments to describe actions or explain a rejection. For further discussion, comment “requires further discussion”; for missing information, comment “the change draft is missing relevant information.” For a rejection, comment “the change does not get endorsed” and explain why. Coordinate an ad-hoc Plasma OpU CRB with the Chair when needed.

If endorsed with actions, Change Owners and Quality Approvers should include those actions when initiating the change control in TrackWise. If rejected due to missing information, provide the missing information and resubmit.

For a Global Product Launch CRB outcome, contact Jeanette Fait, the Global Product Launch CRB Chair, at jeanette.fait@takeda.com. For Bio OpU PCP relevance or a Site CRB change affecting Bio products, share the change information with the respective Bio OpU PQLs.
